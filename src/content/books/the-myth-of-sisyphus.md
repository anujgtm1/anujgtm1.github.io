The essay that sparked my own post on Sisyphus. What stays with me is not gloom or resignation, but clarity: once you stop waiting for the universe to hand you a neat script, every deliberate choice — pushing the stone, noticing the mountain air, building something worthwhile — belongs entirely to you.
