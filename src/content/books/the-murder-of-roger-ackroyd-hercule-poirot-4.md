I went into this book being quite skeptical. A friend of one said that this is like none of which I've ever read. I'm quite glad to agree with him. 
A doctor narrates out the story of the murder and aftermath of the murder of his good friend, Mr. Roger Ackroyd in the small town of King's Abott. The excellent Belgian detective Hercule Poirot gets involved in this unreal course of events.
Let's just say I know of no better way this mystery might have turned out.
