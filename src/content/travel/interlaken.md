Few places make moving between valleys feel as effortless as the Bernese Oberland. You step off a lake ferry, board a mountain train that climbs past cowbells and timber chalets, and forty minutes later you are standing eye-level with hanging glaciers.

## Favorite stretch

The footpath from **Grütschalp to Mürren** stays carved in memory: Eiger, Mönch, and Jungfrau rising straight across the valley while the afternoon clouds slowly lift off the granite walls.
