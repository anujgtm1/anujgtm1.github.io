Iceland rearranges your sense of scale within twenty minutes of leaving Keflavik. Moss-covered lava fields stretch toward low clouds, waterfalls pour off sheer basalt walls right next to the highway, and the light at 11:30 PM looks like a calm amber afternoon.

## Notes from the road

1. **Pack for four seasons in one afternoon.** Bright sun, sideways mist, and wind gusts can trade places over a single cup of coffee.
2. **Stop at every neighborhood pool.** Municipal geothermal pools are the true living rooms of Iceland — warm water, cold air, and unhurried conversation.
3. **Pull over whenever the light shifts.** Half the best moments happened on gravel pullouts that were never on the map.
