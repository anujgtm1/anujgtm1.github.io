Chicago has the rare combination of monumental skyline ambition and deeply grounded neighborhood warmth. Walking out of the Art Institute onto Michigan Avenue or ducking into the winding wooden stacks of Myopic Books never gets old.

## What makes it homey

- **The Lakefront Trail** turns an inland city into a coastal horizon.
- **The architecture tour** is mandatory even on your fifth visit — every bridge and skyscraper carries a century of structural engineering stories.
- **Neighborhood coffee & food** from Pilsen to Logan Square makes every weekend feel like a mini-trip.
