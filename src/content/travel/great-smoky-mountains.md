Doing a PhD in Knoxville came with an extraordinary backyard. Whenever a research problem refused to budge, an hour's drive south put you underneath old-growth hemlocks and layered blue ridges that immediately restored perspective.

## Trail notes

The climb up **Alum Cave to Mount LeConte** in mid-October remains my benchmark day hike — steep stone steps, cool ridge wind, and endless amber ridges folding into the horizon.
