Tokyo rewards the walker who ignores the main avenues and turns one block inward. Two minutes off a neon intersection, the traffic drops to a whisper and you find a six-seat kissaten pouring hand-drip coffee beside a tiny neighborhood shrine.

## Field observations

- **Transit as civic art.** Watching the Yamanote Line run with second-level precision makes any infrastructure engineer quietly envious.
- **Kiyosumi-Shirakawa at 8 AM.** Calm canals, crisp November air, and roasters taking their time over every pour.
- **Yanaka Ginza at dusk.** Wooden shopfronts, warm street lanterns, and the feeling of stepping sideways in time without ever leaving the metropolis.

> The best part of Tokyo is not the scale — it is how much care fits inside six square meters.
